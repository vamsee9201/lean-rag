# Confirmatory Gemini 3.8 Flash versus Qwen3.5 9B protocol

## Execution status

Generation and both automated blind-judge tracks are complete: 250 answers per
model and 500 ratings per judge. Automated findings are reported in
`CONFIRMATORY_RESULTS.md`. The preregistered primary human audit and rating step
is pending; its blinded materials are in `data/confirmatory/review/`.

## Primary question

With the 500-document corpus, BM25 top-five retrieval, prompt, and generation
settings fixed, does Gemini 3.8 Flash improve answer correctness over the local
Qwen3.5 9B model by at least five percentage points?

## Frozen design

- Models: Gemini 3.8 Flash and Qwen3.5 9B only.
- Benchmark: 250 fresh questions generated by the excluded Qwen3.5 2B model.
- Pilot separation: no confirmatory gold page may overlap a pilot gold page.
- Categories: 100 direct, 50 numeric/date, 50 same-document multi-passage,
  25 cross-document, and 25 unanswerable.
- Retrieval: one frozen SQLite FTS5/BM25 top-five result per question.
- Generation: the same evidence and system instruction, temperature 0,
  reasoning/thinking off, and a 220-token output cap.
- Unit of analysis: question. Each question is paired across both models.

## Outcomes and decision rule

The primary outcome is blinded human answer correctness on a 0/1/2 rubric,
normalized to 0/0.5/1. Two reviewers should score every answer independently;
disagreements are adjudicated without model identity. Citation correctness is a
separate binary measure.

The primary effect is the paired mean of Gemini minus Qwen normalized correctness.
Report a two-sided 95% paired bootstrap confidence interval over questions. A
claim that Gemini is practically better requires both:

1. the confidence interval excludes zero; and
2. the estimated improvement is at least five percentage points.

If the interval includes zero, conclude that the experiment did not establish a
difference. If it excludes zero but the effect is below five points, conclude
that the difference is statistically detectable but not practically important.

Automated Qwen and Gemini blind judges, exact-answer checks, abstention accuracy,
citation recall, latency, and retrieval metrics are secondary outcomes. Automated
judges do not replace the primary human result.

## Audit requirements

Every answerable question must have exact source quotes and document/page IDs.
The 25 unanswerable questions were proposed locally and checked against the full
scoped document by Gemini, but a human must confirm absence before final analysis.
Question wording, references, and source evidence must be audited without viewing
finalist answers.
